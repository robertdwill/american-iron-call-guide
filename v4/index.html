Step 6 · minutes 24–27

Transition + Belief Check

Ask permission before you explain

Based on what you've told me, would it be okay if I walked you through how this usually goes from here?
The order only goes one direction. A lender can't fund a guess, and a builder can't price a guess. Design comes first because it's the only thing that produces a real number.
Belief check
(skip if they already said it in their own words):
Would it feel fair to say that having a complete, engineered plan set in hand before you sign a construction contract or pay for dirt would make this a lot easier, and a lot safer for your family?
One thing so there's no surprise. We're a barndo design company, not the builder. We do the plans, and we connect you with builders, barndo lenders and land agents, so you control your vision and your bids.
You get a complete design: architectural and engineering, with the documents your permit office and lender ask for. Preliminary design comes first, then final plans. Take the finished plans to our builder network or any builder you want, and have them bid the same drawings. Our team helps you line up those bids.
The plans are yours. Use any builder you want.

Step 10

Three Endings

There is no fourth

Paid
— deposit or prelim cleared on the call.
Dated
— specific date and time on the calendar, with an invite sent to everyone who decides.
When we talk [DAY], how do you want to handle the design fee?
Disqualified
— you told them straight it's not a fit and why, or they said a real no. Write down the reason.
